# Privacy policy

**Effective October 29, 2026**

recipi is a free, open-source recipe app for iPhone and iPad, made by
Justin Tahara. This policy is short because recipi collects nothing.

## The short version

- recipi doesn't collect, store or share any of your data.
- There's no recipi server and no account.
- There are no analytics, crash reporting, ads or tracking.
- Your recipes are never used to train AI models or anything else.

## Where your recipes are kept

Your recipes, photos, tags, grocery list and cook history are stored on your
device. If iCloud sync is on, they're also kept in your own **private**
iCloud database, which only you can read. The developer of recipi can't see
it. Sync is on by default; you can turn it off in **Settings → Sync**, and
the change applies the next time you open recipi.

## What leaves your device

Only things you start, and only to the place they're meant for:

- **Pages you import.** When you add a recipe from a link, recipi downloads
  that page and the recipe's photo directly from the website. The site sees
  an ordinary visit, the same as opening the link in Safari. For pages that
  only build their recipe with JavaScript, recipi opens the page in a hidden
  browser that keeps no cookies and is thrown away afterwards, so the page's
  own scripts run as they would in any browser. Reading the recipe happens
  on your device.
- **iCloud sync**, through Apple, to your private iCloud database.
- **Translation.** If you translate a recipe, iOS may download the language
  from Apple. Translating happens on your device.
- **Reminders.** If you send a grocery list to Reminders, recipi adds it
  there after asking your permission. Reminders then syncs the way you've
  set it up.
- **Backups.** An exported backup goes wherever you choose to save it.

Apple's handling of iCloud, Translation and Reminders is covered by
[Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## What recipi never does

- Collect personal information, usage data, identifiers or diagnostics.
- Track you across apps or websites.
- Show ads or include any third-party code. recipi uses only Apple's
  frameworks, and its [source code](https://github.com/justin-tahara/recipi)
  is public, so anyone can check.
- Sell or share your data. It has none to share.

## Deleting your data

Delete a recipe in the app and it's gone from your device and, with sync on,
from your iCloud. To remove everything, delete the app, and if you used
sync, remove recipi's data in **Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage
Account Storage**.

## Children

recipi collects no data from anyone, including children.
