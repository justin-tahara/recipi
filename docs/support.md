# Support

recipi is made by one person in their spare time. If something's wrong or
you have a question, the [GitHub issues](https://github.com/justin-tahara/recipi/issues)
are the best place to ask.

## A recipe didn't import

If recipi says a site didn't let it read the page, open the page in Safari
and share it to recipi instead: recipi then reads the page Safari already
loaded.

Some sites mark up their recipes in unusual ways. Please
[report the site](https://github.com/justin-tahara/recipi/issues/new?template=site-import.yml)
with the link and what went wrong (nothing came in, or something was
missing or wrong). In the meantime you can add the recipe by hand.

## How do backups work?

In **Settings → Backup**, choose **Export library** to save every recipe,
with its photos, notes, tags and cook history, as one `.zip` file. You can
keep it in Files, iCloud Drive, Google Drive, Dropbox or anywhere else the
Files picker reaches. Nothing is uploaded unless the place you save to does
it.

**Restore from backup** reads a backup back in. It only adds recipes you
don't already have, so it's safe to restore onto a library that already has
recipes. Each recipe in the backup is a standard schema.org file other apps
can read; see the [backup format](backup-format.md).

## How does iCloud sync work?

recipi keeps your recipes in step across your iPhone and iPad through your
own private iCloud database. You need to be signed in to iCloud on each
device. Sync is on by default.

To turn it off, go to **Settings → Sync** and switch off **iCloud sync**,
then close recipi and open it again. Your recipes stay on the device;
turning sync off doesn't delete what's already in iCloud.

New changes can take a few minutes to show up on your other devices.

## Does recipi collect my data?

No. There's no account, no server, no analytics and no tracking. See the
[privacy policy](privacy.md).

## Something else is broken, or I have an idea

- [Report a bug](https://github.com/justin-tahara/recipi/issues/new?template=bug.yml)
- [Suggest a feature](https://github.com/justin-tahara/recipi/issues/new?template=feature.yml)
- For a security problem, please
  [report it privately](https://github.com/justin-tahara/recipi/security/advisories/new)
  instead of opening a public issue.
