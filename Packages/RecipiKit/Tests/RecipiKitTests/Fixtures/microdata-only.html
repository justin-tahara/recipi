Classic Buttermilk Biscuits

Tall, flaky biscuits with a crisp top.

  • Prep: 15 mins
  • Cook: 12 minutes
  • Total: 27 min
  • Makes: 10 biscuits
Reader rating 4.8

Ingredients

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 tablespoon baking powder
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 6 tablespoons cold butter
  • 3/4 cup cold buttermilk

Method

  1. Whisk the flour, baking powder and salt.
  2. Cut in the butter until pea-sized.
  3. Stir in the buttermilk, pat out, fold twice and cut.
  4. Bake at 450°F for 12 minutes.
