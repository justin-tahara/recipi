Weeknight Chicken Enchiladas

8 enchiladas

Ingredients

Red sauce

  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 2 tablespoons flour
  • 2 tablespoons chili powder
  • 2 cups chicken stock

Filling

  • 3 cups shredded cooked chicken
  • 8 flour tortillas
  • 2 cups grated cheddar

Instructions

Sauce

  • Cook the oil, flour and chili powder for 1 minute.
  • Whisk in the stock and simmer until thickened, about 5 minutes.

Assembly

  • Fill each tortilla with chicken and a little cheese and roll it up.
  • Lay them in a dish, cover with sauce and the rest of the cheese.
  • Bake at 400°F for 20 minutes.
