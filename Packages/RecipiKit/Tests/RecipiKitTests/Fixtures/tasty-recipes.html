Brown Butter Chocolate Chip Cookies

  • Yield: 24 cookies

Ingredients

  • 1 cup (225g) unsalted butter
  • 1 cup (200g) brown sugar
  • 1/2 cup (100g) granulated sugar
  • 2 large eggs
  • 2 teaspoons vanilla
  • 2 1/4 cups (280g) flour
  • 1 teaspoon baking soda
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 2 cups chocolate chips

Instructions

  1. Brown the butter in a saucepan and let it cool for 15 minutes.
  2. Beat in both sugars, then the eggs and vanilla.
  3. Stir in the flour, baking soda and salt, then the chocolate.
  4. Scoop onto lined sheets and bake at 350°F for 10 to 12 minutes.
