Grandma’s Apple Pie

Servings 8 slices

Pastry

  • 2 ½ cups flour
  • 1 cup cold butter cubed
  • 6 tablespoons ice water

Filling

  • 7 tart apples, sliced
  • ¾ cup sugar
  • 1 teaspoon cinnamon

  • Cut the butter into the flour until it looks like coarse crumbs.

    Stir in the water.

  • Chill the dough for an hour, then roll out two crusts.
  • Toss the apples with the sugar and cinnamon and pile into the bottom crust.
  • Cover, crimp, cut vents and bake at 425°F for 45 minutes.
