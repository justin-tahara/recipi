Sheet Pan Gnocchi with Sausage and Peppers

4 servings

Ingredients

Scale

For the pan

  • 1 pound shelf-stable gnocchi
  • 12 ounces Italian sausage, sliced
  • 2 bell peppers, cut into strips
  • 1 red onion, cut into wedges
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil

To finish

  • 1/4 cup grated parmesan
  • A handful of basil leaves

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 425°F.
  2. Toss the gnocchi, sausage, peppers and onion with the oil on a sheet pan.
  3. Roast for 25 minutes, stirring halfway.
  4. Scatter with parmesan and basil.
