Red Lentil Soup

Serves 6

1 tablespoon olive oil

1 onion, diced

2 carrots, diced

1 1/2 cups red lentils

6 cups stock

Juice of 1 lemon

Soften the onion and carrots in the oil for 8 minutes.

Add the lentils and stock and simmer for 20 minutes.

Blend half the soup and stir it back in.

Finish with the lemon juice and season.
